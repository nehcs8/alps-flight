# Alps Flight

A 3-D terrain viewer of the Alps for phones: fly over the mountains, with hiking paths, roads, tunnels and names.
Open **https://nehcs8.github.io/alps-flight/** in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome, then *Share → Add to Home Screen*.
Areas you have looked at once stay available offline.

Controls: left stick = move, one finger = look, two fingers = fly forward / back, round buttons on the right =
height, speed, time and date, map, settings (paths & roads, names, info, quality).

Not for navigation: trail grades in OpenStreetMap are often missing or lower than the official marking, and no data
here shows snow, closures or current conditions.
